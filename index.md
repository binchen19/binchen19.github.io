Hi! I am an Assistant Professor at the [School of Future Media](https://hkums.hku.hk/), [the University of Hong Kong](https://hku.hk/), where I also serve as Director of the [BJ/BJMAI Program](https://hkums.hku.hk/bjmai/). My research explores digital platforms and politics, AI and media studies, computational social science, and comparative communication studies. Before joining HKU, I received my Ph.D. in [Journalism and Media](https://journalism.utexas.edu/) and M.S. in [Statistics and Data Science](https://stat.utexas.edu/) from [the University of Texas at Austin](https://www.utexas.edu/).

I am recruiting research assistants and PhD students; see [Prospective Students & RAs](#join).

## Prospective Students & RAs

**Research Assistants** — I'm looking for RAs in Computational Social Science. Strong skills in statistics and programming (Python or R) are required. If you're interested, please email me your CV.

**PhD Applicants** — I am currently considering inquiries only from applicants with strong quantitative or computational skills, a solid grounding in statistics, and research interests aligned with computational social science. Please review [SFM’s guidelines](https://hkums.hku.hk/master-of-philosophy-doctor-of-philosophy-in-journalism/) and apply through [HKU’s application portal](https://gradsch.hku.hk/prospective_students/application/how_to_apply/online_application_system).
